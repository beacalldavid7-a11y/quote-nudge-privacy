Quote Nudge Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

Developer: David Beacall (Beacall Digital)

Quote Nudge helps tradespeople follow up the quotes they have sent. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

1. What the app stores. The app stores what you type in: customer names, mobile numbers, job descriptions, prices, quote and follow-up dates, quote status and message wording. All of it is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We have no server or account system. We do not receive, collect or see your data.

2. No tracking. The app has no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools. It does not sell or share any data.

3. Messages. When you choose Follow up by SMS or WhatsApp, the app opens your own Messages or WhatsApp app with the message filled in. You choose whether to press Send. Quote Nudge never sends messages itself. Anything you send is handled by your carrier or by WhatsApp under their own privacy policies.

4. Contacts. The app does not access your contacts or address book. You type in each customer's name and number yourself.

5. Notifications. If you allow notifications, the app schedules follow-up reminders on your device. They are created on the phone, not sent from a server. On the lock screen they do not show customer details. You can turn notifications off in Android settings, and the app will still show due quotes when you open it.

6. Backups. You can export a backup file (JSON) and import it again. The file contains your customer data. It goes only where you choose to save or share it, so please keep it safe. Importing a backup replaces the quotes on the device.

7. Deleting your data. You can delete individual quotes in the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes all its data from the device. We hold no copy. Export a backup first if you want to keep your quotes.

Security. Your data stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox and your device lock. Backup files you export are not encrypted, so store them somewhere safe.

8. Children. The app is meant for adult tradespeople and is not aimed at children.

9. Changes. If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.

10. Contact. Questions about this policy or the app: reelfishingapps@gmail.com
